## Systems and data

| Book | Author | Read it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* | Martin Kleppmann | Replication, partitioning, transactions and streams, in depth: the best companion to Parts III and V |
| *Database Internals* | Alex Petrov | How storage engines and distributed databases actually work |
| *Release It!* | Michael T. Nygard | Stability patterns and anti-patterns from production failures (Chapter 37) |
| *Site Reliability Engineering* | Google (Beyer et al.) | SLOs, error budgets, on-call and postmortems (Chapter 43) |
| *Enterprise Integration Patterns* | Gregor Hohpe, Bobby Woolf | The vocabulary of messaging (Chapters 33–34) |

## Architecture and design

| Book | Author | Read it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Domain-Driven Design* | Eric Evans | The original: bounded contexts and the model (Chapter 29) |
| *Implementing Domain-Driven Design* | Vaughn Vernon | Aggregates and events in practice |
| *Building Microservices* | Sam Newman | Service boundaries, data ownership and migration (Chapter 31) |
| *Monolith to Microservices* | Sam Newman | Incremental decomposition and the strangler fig |
| *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture* | Martin Fowler | Repositories, unit of work, and the patterns ORMs implement |
| *Architecture Patterns with Python* | Harry Percival, Bob Gregory | Ports and adapters, events and CQRS in Python |
| *Accelerate* | Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim | The research behind delivery practices (Chapter 42) |

- [ASP.NET Core](https://learn.microsoft.com/aspnet/core/) and [Entity Framework Core](https://learn.microsoft.com/ef/core/)
- [Spring Boot](https://docs.spring.io/spring-boot/) and [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/home.html)
- [Go](https://go.dev/doc/) and [Effective Go](https://go.dev/doc/effective_go)
- [Python](https://docs.python.org/3/), [Django](https://docs.djangoproject.com/) and [FastAPI](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/)
